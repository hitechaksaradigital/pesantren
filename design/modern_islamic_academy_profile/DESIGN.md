## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the synthesis of traditional Islamic values and forward-looking academic excellence. Designed for a modern Islamic boarding school (pesantren modern), the identity balances dignity, intellectual clarity, and approachable warmth. The visual language conveys trustworthy stewardship, pedagogical rigor, and spiritual serenity.

The chosen aesthetic fuses **Modern Academic Minimalism** with subtle **Islamic Geometric Restraint**. It avoids ornate clichés, instead using generous negative space, sophisticated deep botanical hues, tactile card surfaces, and serene ivory backdrops to evoke the quiet contemplation of a scholarly sanctuary. Micro-interactions are calm and purposeful, instilling confidence in prospective parents, scholars, and institutional partners.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical scholarly ink, deep evergreen courtyard foliage, and stone arcades.

- **Primary (`#092328` - Midnight Teal):** The foundational anchor representing institutional authority, dignity, and intellectual depth. Used for dominant headings, primary buttons, footers, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Secondary (`#12544F` - Deep Forest Teal):** Represents continuity and academic tradition. Used for interactive hover states, secondary actions, active navigation anchors, and rich informational cards.
- **Tertiary (`#2A835F` - Vital Emerald):** Represents renewal, youth, and vibrant campus life. Applied to primary calls-to-action (registration/admission badges), key data metrics, and verified credentials.
- **Neutral Accent (`#8BBB92` - Soft Sage):** A soothing bridge tone used for delicate borders, light tag backgrounds, highlight fills, and subtle contextual borders.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base Background: `#F9FAF8` (Soft Ivory Canvas)
  - Card & Modal Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Scholastic White)
  - Tinted Surface Alternate: `#F0F4F1` (Parchment Tint for segmented content bands, curriculum grids, and table footers)
  - Border Subdued: `rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.12)`

## Typography

The typography couples the humanized geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent architectural structures with the utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for dense academic curriculums and long-form prose.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with negative tracking to produce tight, confident editorial titles reminiscent of esteemed collegiate journals.
- **Body Text:** Set with open line heights to promote unhurried comprehension across program descriptions, mission statements, and administrative schedules.
- **Arabic Script Integration:** Where Quranic verses or Hadith quotations appear, render them using an appropriate traditional Naskh or Amiri web typeface at 125% of the surrounding Latin text size with generous line height (minimum 1.9) to preserve diacritical clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded by a maximum content container of `1280px` for desktop screens, ensuring focused readability on ultrawide monitors.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `margin-mobile` of 1.25rem, `gutter-mobile` of 1rem)
  - Tablet: `768px – 1024px` (8 columns, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, `margin` of 3rem, `gutter` of 1.5rem)
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` on desktop) prevents dense educational catalogs from feeling congested. Alternating section bands between `#F9FAF8` and `#F0F4F1` establish structured thematic divisions (e.g., from Board of Asatidz to Kurikulum Tahfizh).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **organic tonal layers** paired with **tinted ambient shadows**, avoiding artificial stark grays.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F9FAF8` or `#F0F4F1` with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Navigation Bars):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a subtle teal-tinted atmospheric shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.03)` and a crisp low-contrast perimeter border `1px solid rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Featured Programs):** Raised card state via `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Navigational Headers):** Layered depth with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at `rgba(249, 250, 248, 0.92)` supported by `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The system implements `roundedness: 2` (base radius of `0.5rem` / `8px`), lending a civilized, gentle curve that feels warm without becoming playful or childish.

- Standard buttons, form fields, and dropdown elements: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Program cards, feature containers, and article blocks: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Prominent hero callout banners, testimonials, and registration modals: `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Badges, status chips, and admission deadline pills: Fully rounded pill shape (`9999px`).
- Geometric motifs: Subtle arch-inspired top corners (`border-top-left-radius: 2rem; border-top-right-radius: 2rem`) may be applied sparingly to feature photography frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Teal `#092328` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#12544F`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem` (`space-sm` + `space-md`), border-radius `0.5rem`.
- **Accent (Registration / Admission CTA):** Emerald `#2A835F` fill, white text, subtle elevation. Hover translates upward by 1px with deepened shadow.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent surface with a `1.5px` border in `#12544F`, text `#12544F`. On hover, fills with `rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.06)`.
- **Text Link:** `#12544F` text with an underline offset of 4px that animates on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Academic & Status Badges:** Pill-shaped, `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Fill in `rgba(139, 187, 146, 0.2)` with `#12544F` label text.
- **Admission Open Indicator:** Emerald `#2A835F` background tint with a pulsing circular indicator dot (`#2A835F`).

### Cards (Program, Curricula & News)
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.08)`, and `1rem` corner rounding.
- Inner padding: `1.5rem`. Top imagery incorporates an aspect ratio of 16:10 with soft interior clipping.
- Academic Program Cards include a category tag at top-left, a concise headline, a 3-point feature checklist with emerald checkmarks, and an explicit action link.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- Text inputs and dropdowns feature an ivory tint `#FFFFFF`, surrounded by a 1px border in `rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.16)`.
- Active focus state: Border shifts to `#2A835F` with an external glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(42, 131, 95, 0.2)`.
- Labels are rendered in `label-md` using `#092328` with supplementary instructions in `#12544F` (60% opacity).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square rounded (`4px`) for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#12544F`. Checked: Filled with `#2A835F` displaying a crisp white glyph.

### Navigation Header & Mega Menu
- Desktop sticky bar using semi-translucent Ivory (`rgba(249, 250, 248, 0.94)`) with subtle lower border `rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.08)`.
- Dropdown menus organize pesantren branches (Tahfizh, Kitab Kuning, Kurikulum Sains & Bahasa) into clean multi-column cards with icon highlights in `#8BBB92`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Hadith / Ayah Callout Block:** Centered or side-bordered quote panel featuring `#F0F4F1` fill, an emerald left-border accent (`4px` in `#2A835F`), traditional Arabic script at the top, and structured Indonesian translation beneath.
- **Asatidz (Faculty) Profile Card:** Portrait image with subtle arched header, scholar name, alma mater credential, and specialization chip.
- **Admission Stepper:** Clean 4-step horizontal tracker using `#2A835F` for completed stages, `#12544F` for the active step, and soft sage `#8BBB92` for upcoming phases.
